# Playtest Instructions

You're looking at a canvas of 6 animated programs. Each one is its own
living visualization — a short piece of code that draws something.
Your job is to breed them together and see what evolves.

---

## The basics

**Click a node** to select it (it glows orange — that's Parent 1).
**Click a second node** to select it too (it glows blue — that's Parent 2).
**Click BREED.** A child node appears below them, combining code from both parents.

That's it. Keep going.

---

## Things to try

1. **Breed two visually opposite nodes** — e.g. something sparse and something
   dense. See what comes out.

2. **Breed a child with one of its parents.** Grandchildren tend to lean toward
   whichever side you keep selecting.

3. **Click ANIMATE** (with one node selected) to bring a static node to life
   as a real-time animation.

4. **Click GENERATE ×3** to inject 3 fresh random nodes if you want new
   starting material.

5. **Delete nodes you don't like** — click one to select it, then press the
   Delete key. Clean up as you go.

---

## Notes

- The code panel under each canvas shows the program that made it.
  You don't need to read it — just use it as a fingerprint to tell nodes apart.
- Nodes don't move unless you drag them. Feel free to rearrange.
- RESET wipes everything and starts fresh with 6 new random seeds.
- There's no score, no goal. Just explore.
